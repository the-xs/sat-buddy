# SAT Practice Questions - Math

Question 1: If x + 5 = 12, what is the value of x?
A) 5
B) 7
C) 12
D) 17
Answer: B

Explanation: To solve for x, subtract 5 from both sides: x = 12 - 5 = 7

Question 2: What is 15% of 200?
A) 15
B) 30
C) 45
D) 60
Answer: B

Explanation: 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30

Question 3: If a triangle has sides of length 3, 4, and 5, what type of triangle is it?
A) Equilateral
B) Isosceles
C) Right
D) Obtuse
Answer: C

Explanation: Since 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5², this satisfies the Pythagorean theorem, making it a right triangle.

Question 4: What is the slope of a line passing through points (2, 3) and (6, 11)?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: B

Explanation: Slope = (y₂ - y₁)/(x₂ - x₁) = (11 - 3)/(6 - 2) = 8/4 = 2

Question 5: If f(x) = 2x + 3, what is f(5)?
A) 10
B) 11
C) 13
D) 15
Answer: C

Explanation: f(5) = 2(5) + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13

Question 6: What is the area of a circle with radius 4?
A) 8π
B) 12π
C) 16π
D) 20π
Answer: C

Explanation: Area = πr² = π(4)² = 16π

Question 7: Solve for y: 3y - 7 = 14
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 8
Answer: C

Explanation: 3y = 14 + 7 = 21, so y = 21/3 = 7

Question 8: What is the value of 2³ × 2²?
A) 16
B) 32
C) 64
D) 128
Answer: B

Explanation: 2³ × 2² = 2⁵ = 32

Question 9: If a rectangle has length 8 and width 5, what is its perimeter?
A) 13
B) 26
C) 40
D) 52
Answer: B

Explanation: Perimeter = 2(length + width) = 2(8 + 5) = 2(13) = 26

Question 10: What is the median of the set {3, 7, 2, 9, 5}?
A) 3
B) 5
C) 7
D) 9
Answer: B

Explanation: First sort the numbers: {2, 3, 5, 7, 9}. The median is the middle value: 5
